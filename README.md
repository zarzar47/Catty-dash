Catty Dash
Catty Dash is a mobile game developed for the Mindstorm Labs Summer Game Jam. The player controls a ninja cat on a mission to retrieve its stolen fish from a gang of dogs. The game includes 9 levels where the player must navigate through the dog gang's base, facing various obstacles and enemies.

Technical Information:
Engine: Developed in Unity.
3D Assets: Modeled and animated using Blender.
Audio: Sound effects and music tracks created with Audacity.
Controls: The game utilizes touch and swipe-based controls optimized for mobile platforms.
Features:
9 Levels: Explore diverse environments and defeat enemies across nine challenging levels.
Custom Assets: All 3D models, animations, and audio were developed in-house.
Mobile Optimized: Designed specifically for mobile devices with touch-based interactions.
Installation:
